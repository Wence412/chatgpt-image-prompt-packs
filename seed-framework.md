# S.E.E.D. Framework

Use this structure for professional image prompts.

## Subject
The hero object, person, scene, product, interface, or campaign asset.

## Elements
Props, materials, visual cues, colors, outfit, product packaging, typography area, or icons.

## Environment
Studio, lifestyle scene, office, retail shelf, product surface, futuristic setting, website layout, or platform context.

## Details
Lens, lighting, angle, depth of field, illustration style, realism level, output constraints, and brand rules.

## Production line
Create a clean, commercially usable, brand-safe image with strong composition, realistic proportions, accurate lighting, no random text, no distorted logos, no extra fingers, no clutter, and no split-screen or collage layout unless requested.
